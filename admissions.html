Admissions

We welcome applications from parents seeking quality education for their children in a safe, disciplined and God-fearing environment.


Admission Requirements

  • Birth Certificate
  • Passport Photograph
  • Previous School Report
  • Completed Admission Form

Admission Process

  1. Obtain an Admission Form.
  2. Complete and submit the form.
  3. Provide the required documents.
  4. Attend an assessment (if applicable).
  5. Receive admission confirmation.

Age Requirements

  • Crèche: 6 months and above
  • Nursery: 2 – 5 years
  • Primary: 5 years and above

Available Classes

  • Crèche
  • Nursery
  • Primary
